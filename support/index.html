Rubik Orbit — Support

A 3×3 cube puzzle shown only as its flat circle diagram.

How to play

Every coloured dot is a sticker of the cube. Drag along an outer circle to turn that face: its 12 dots slide three places and the 3×3 cluster inside it spins. Drag a dashed middle circle to slide a middle row. Make every cluster one colour to solve it.

Hints

Start a puzzle with Hints on and tap Hint: arrows on the board show the next turn and the pill tells you how many moves are left. Hints always work from your current position, even after mistakes. Puzzles started as No hints · ranked post your time to Game Center.

Frequently asked

My puzzle is gone? The app saves automatically. If the title screen shows Continue, your puzzle is still there.

Leaderboards are empty. Only solves finished without ever looking at a hint are submitted, and you need to be signed in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app.

No sound? Check the Sound effects toggle in Settings on the title screen, and the silent switch on your phone.
