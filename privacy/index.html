Rubik Orbit — Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

Short version: Rubik Orbit does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party software in the app. The app itself makes no network requests.

What stays on your device

  • Your current puzzle, move count and clock, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics and the last difficulty you chose.

This information is stored only on your device (and in your own device backup, if you have iCloud or computer backups turned on). It never reaches us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Rubik Orbit submits your solve times for puzzles played without hints to Game Center leaderboards, and can show Apple's leaderboard screen. This is provided by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your Game Center identity, and you can play without Game Center at all.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
